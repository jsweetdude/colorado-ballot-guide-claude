# Email to campaigns: the 25 statements

Written 2026-10-16 for John to send himself. One message per campaign; the addresses are below. Neutral by design: it names no measure positions and asks for the candidate's own answers.

---

**Subject:** Colorado Ballot Guide: your positions on 25 statements, by October 22

Hello,

I run the Colorado Ballot Guide (https://colorado-ballot-guide.vercel.app), a free, nonpartisan voter guide for the November 3 general election. It shows voters how closely each candidate's sourced positions line up with their own answers to 25 statements, and it links every position to a public source.

Right now the guide knows more about candidates with voting records than about everyone else, which is not fair to challengers. I would like to publish your campaign's own answers so voters see your positions in your words.

Could you answer the 25 statements below by Wednesday, October 22? For each one, reply with a number from 1 to 5:

1 = strongly disagree
2 = disagree
3 = neither agree nor disagree, or mixed
4 = agree
5 = strongly agree

You may also skip any statement, or add a sentence of explanation. Your answers will be published as a questionnaire response, quoted as you wrote them, with the date, on the candidate's profile page. Nothing is scored or ranked against other candidates unless at least two candidates in the race have enough known positions.

1. Colorado should tax higher incomes at higher rates instead of using one flat rate for everyone.
2. State law should cap Colorado's income tax rate at today's 4.4 percent.
3. When Colorado collects more than its TABOR revenue limit, the state should keep the extra for schools instead of refunding it.
4. Sales tax collected on sporting goods should be set aside for water, land and wildfire programs.
5. The state constitution should protect the right to buy and use natural gas in homes and businesses.
6. Government should speed up the shift from oil, gas and coal to renewable energy.
7. The state should be able to override local zoning rules to allow more housing to be built.
8. Government should do more to make sure everyone has health insurance, even if it costs taxpayers more.
9. The federal government should keep broad tariffs on imported goods.
10. Congress was right to pass the 2025 federal tax and spending law known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
11. Federal public lands should stay in federal ownership rather than being sold or transferred.
12. Abortion should be legal in all or most cases.
13. Gun laws should be stricter than they are now.
14. Students should play on school sports teams that match their biological sex, not their gender identity.
15. Colorado should ban surgery that changes the sex characteristics of patients under 18 as treatment for gender identity.
16. Colorado police and jails should notify federal immigration authorities when they charge someone with a serious crime and cannot confirm the person is in the country legally.
17. Deporting people who are in the U.S. without legal status should be a top federal priority.
18. Fentanyl crimes should carry tougher penalties, including mandatory prison time for selling any amount.
19. Child sex trafficking should be punished with life in prison without parole.
20. Hunting and fishing should be a right protected in the state constitution.
21. Voters should have to write an ID number, such as the last four digits of their Social Security number, on their mail ballot envelope.
22. Ballot questions should be required to be written at an eighth-grade reading level.
23. Only Colorado's independent commission should redraw the state's congressional map, even if other states redraw theirs mid-decade.
24. Colorado's all-mail ballot election system should stay largely as it is.
25. Colorado's state government should challenge the current federal administration's policies in court.

Please reply from a campaign address so I can attribute the answers. If you would rather point me to a published questionnaire or statement that already covers some of these, that works too.

Thank you,

John Sweet
Colorado Ballot Guide
